Chunky pill button with a 3D edge that squishes on press; use for every call to action.
```jsx
<Button iconEnd="arrow-right">Create a story</Button>
<Button variant="secondary" iconStart="play-circle">Watch how</Button>
<Button variant="outline" size="sm">Maybe later</Button>
```
- One `primary` (yellow) per view. `secondary` (blue) for the second action, `outline`/`ghost` for tertiary.
- `night` on light-on-dark bedtime screens; `cherry` for sale/limited offers or destructive confirms.
