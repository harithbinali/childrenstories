Labelled text field (or textarea) for sign-up, checkout and the story prompt.
```jsx
<Input label="Child's name" placeholder="e.g. Layla" iconStart="smiley" />
<Input label="What happens in the story?" multiline placeholder="A dragon who is scared of the dark…" />
<Input label="Email" error="That email looks a little wobbly" />
```
- Errors are kind and specific; never "Invalid input".
