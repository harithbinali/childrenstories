Modal for confirmations and celebrations (story ready, purchase complete, delete).
```jsx
<Dialog open={open} onClose={close} icon="confetti" title="Your story is ready!" actions={<Button iconEnd="book-open">Start reading</Button>}>
  Luna's adventure has 12 brand-new pages.
</Dialog>
```
- Use `icon` medallion for celebratory moments; omit for plain confirms.
